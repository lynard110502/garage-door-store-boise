Before you call

Questions the shop already answers.

When should I replace a garage door?

Replace it if it has not run properly for a while, it is too old, you have had a break-in, it lacks child safety features, or it is badly damaged. Do not try to fix it yourself. Call a trained garage door dealer. For repair in Boise, call (208) 514-2871.

How do I choose a garage door company in Boise?

Ask which services they offer, read testimonials, and ask for references you can contact. Look for signs the company is legitimate and check that it is licensed. For an installation quote, call (208) 514-2871.

Can I install the garage door myself?

No. Installation needs special tools and training. Handled poorly, a new door can be damaged and the safety and security of the home can suffer. Professionals have the tools to do the job. Call (208) 514-2871 for a free installation quote.

What is a garage door tune-up?

A tune-up is the shop’s most affordable way to support how the door runs and how long it lasts. The published full tune-up is $125, tax and labor included. It is meant to head off a larger repair and reduce safety problems with the door.

Why did my garage door stop working?

Causes the shop lists include a blocked photo eye, a remote problem, broken torsion springs, limits that need adjustment, dead transmitter batteries, something in the door’s path, or a track out of alignment. Call a professional for repair in Boise. Springs and cables are not a do-it-yourself job.
